Tulja Bhavani Temple

Tulja Bhavani Temple is one of the most famous temples in Maharashtra. It is located in Tuljapur and dedicated to Goddess Bhavani. Devotees from all over India visit this holy temple to seek blessings. The temple is known for its ancient architecture, spiritual atmosphere, and rich history connected with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Shani Shingnapur

Shani Shingnapur Temple is dedicated to Lord Shani. The village is famous because many houses do not have doors, as people believe Lord Shani protects them from theft. Thousands of devotees visit every Saturday to offer prayers and seek protection from negative effects of Shani.

Siddhivinayak Temple

Siddhivinayak Temple in Mumbai is one of the richest and most visited Ganesh temples in India. Devotees believe Lord Ganesha fulfills wishes and removes obstacles. Celebrities, politicians, and devotees from different parts of the country visit this temple regularly.

Trimbakeshwar Temple

Trimbakeshwar Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. It is located near Nashik and is famous for its spiritual importance. The temple architecture is beautiful and attracts pilgrims throughout the year. The holy Godavari River also originates near this temple.

Grishneshwar Temple

Grishneshwar Temple is another sacred Jyotirlinga temple located near Ellora Caves in Maharashtra. The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is known for its ancient carvings and peaceful environment. Devotees visit this temple to experience divine blessings and spirituality.
