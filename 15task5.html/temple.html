About Pandharpur

Pandharpur is one of the most sacred pilgrimage places in Maharashtra, famous for the divine temple of Lord Vitthal and Goddess Rukmini. Lakhs of devotees visit this holy city every year to seek blessings and participate in spiritual activities.

The temple is situated on the banks of the holy Chandrabhaga River. Pandharpur is especially known for the grand Ashadhi Ekadashi and Kartiki Ekadashi celebrations where devotees perform devotional songs, bhajans, and padayatras with great faith and devotion.

Shri Vitthal is worshipped as a form of Lord Krishna/Vishnu. Devotees believe that visiting Pandharpur and taking darshan of Lord Vitthal brings peace, happiness, and spiritual blessings.
